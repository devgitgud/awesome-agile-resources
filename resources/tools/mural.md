# Mural

## Description

Mural is a digital whiteboard platform for Agile workshops, retrospectives, and collaborative planning sessions.

## Why we recommend it

It provides facilitation-focused collaboration patterns and templates for distributed teams running Agile ceremonies.

## Pros

- Strong workshop facilitation capabilities
- Good template support for retrospectives and planning

## Cons

- Pricing can be a constraint for smaller teams
- Rich boards may require facilitator guidance for clarity

## Best for

Agile facilitators, Scrum Masters, and remote teams running structured workshops.

## Alternatives

- Miro
- FigJam
- Whimsical
