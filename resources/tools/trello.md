# Trello

## Description

Trello is a simple, card-based project management tool that helps teams visualize and track work.

## Why we recommend it

It is easy to adopt and useful for teams starting with Agile or lightweight Kanban practices.

## Pros

- Very easy to learn and use
- Good free tier for small teams

## Cons

- Limited advanced reporting out of the box
- Can require power-ups for complex workflows

## Best for

Small teams and startups that want simple workflow visualization.

## Alternatives

- Jira
- Linear
- Asana
