# Agile Alliance

## Description

A long-running nonprofit advancing Agile values, practices, and education through a trusted community network.

## Why we recommend it

It offers a broad and credible view of Agile practice, including guidance, events, and practitioner resources that remain relevant over time.

## Pros

- Strong community credibility
- Broad coverage of Agile topics and perspectives

## Cons

- Content variety can be overwhelming
- Some material is more conceptual than prescriptive

## Best for

Practitioners seeking a well-established reference point for Agile learning and community engagement.

## Alternatives

- Scrum.org
- Mountain Goat Software
