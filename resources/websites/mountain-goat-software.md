# Mountain Goat Software

## Description

A respected resource for Agile planning, estimation, and coaching guidance from Mike Cohn.

## Why we recommend it

It is especially useful for teams that need practical techniques for planning, prioritization, and adaptive delivery without losing rigor.

## Pros

- Practitioner-oriented guidance
- Strong material on estimation and planning

## Cons

- Less broad than full community platforms
- Some content is more tactical than strategic

## Best for

Product Owners, Scrum Masters, and teams improving planning and delivery discipline.

## Alternatives

- Agile Alliance
- Scrum.org
