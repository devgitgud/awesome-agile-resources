# Scrum Guide

## Description

The Scrum Guide is the official and canonical definition of Scrum by its creators.

## Why we recommend it

It is the primary reference for Scrum events, accountabilities, and commitments.

## Pros

- Official and concise reference
- Regularly updated by Scrum co-creators

## Cons

- Intentionally brief and not prescriptive
- Limited implementation examples

## Best for

Scrum Masters, Product Owners, Developers, and teams aligning on Scrum fundamentals.

## Alternatives

- Evidence-Based Management Guide
- Nexus Guide
