# Agile Foundations

## Description

A practical introduction to Agile mindset, principles, and lightweight implementation strategies.

## Why we recommend it

It is a strong entry point for professionals who need a clear, business-oriented overview of agile adoption without excessive jargon.

## Pros

- Accessible and concise
- Useful for leaders and newcomers to Agile

## Cons

- Less depth than certification-focused training
- Some examples are more general than domain-specific

## Best for

Leaders, managers, and team members seeking a solid introductory grounding in Agile.

## Alternatives

- Professional Scrum Master (PSM I)
- Agile Development Specialization
