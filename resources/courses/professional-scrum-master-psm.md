# Professional Scrum Master (PSM I)

## Description

A rigorous course on Scrum fundamentals, roles, events, and servant leadership from Scrum.org.

## Why we recommend it

It provides a strong foundation for Scrum practitioners who need practical, evidence-based understanding rather than superficial theory.

## Pros

- Excellent for building real Scrum fluency
- Strong alignment with the Scrum Guide and practical facilitation

## Cons

- Requires commitment to study and practice
- Best suited for learners who want depth rather than speed

## Best for

Scrum Masters, team leads, and practitioners preparing for credible Scrum certification.

## Alternatives

- Agile Foundations
- Agile Development Specialization
