# Agile Development Specialization

## Description

A structured specialization covering Agile principles, delivery practices, and iterative software development.

## Why we recommend it

It offers a balanced mix of theory and practical delivery thinking for teams improving their software development rhythm.

## Pros

- Well-structured learning path
- Useful for cross-functional teams and technical leads

## Cons

- Less focused on facilitation than Scrum-specific training
- Some modules are more academic than operational

## Best for

Developers, team leads, and product professionals building agile delivery capability.

## Alternatives

- Professional Scrum Master (PSM I)
- Agile Foundations
