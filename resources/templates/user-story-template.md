# User Story Template

## Description

A standard user story format that captures role, need, and value to improve backlog clarity.

## Why we recommend it

It helps teams keep scope user-centered and supports better refinement conversations.

## Pros

- Improves communication between product and engineering
- Supports clearer acceptance criteria discussions

## Cons

- Overuse can lead to formulaic stories
- Needs complementary discovery and context

## Best for

Product Owners and teams improving backlog quality and story consistency.

## Alternatives

- Job story template
- Impact mapping
