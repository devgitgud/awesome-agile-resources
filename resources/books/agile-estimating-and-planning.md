# Agile Estimating and Planning

## Description

Mike Cohn presents practical estimation and release planning techniques for Agile teams.

## Why we recommend it

It provides structured methods for forecasting and planning while preserving Agile adaptability.

## Pros

- Concrete planning techniques
- Helpful for balancing predictability and flexibility

## Cons

- Some practices need adaptation for modern product teams
- Dense for beginners

## Best for

Product Owners, Scrum Masters, and teams improving planning maturity.

## Alternatives

- Forecasting and Simulations texts
- Team Topologies (for flow-oriented context)
