# Scrum: The Art of Doing Twice the Work in Half the Time

## Description

Jeff Sutherland introduces Scrum concepts and stories showing how iterative delivery can improve outcomes.

## Why we recommend it

It gives approachable, real-world context for Scrum adoption and cross-functional team collaboration.

## Pros

- Easy to read for broad audiences
- Strong storytelling and practical examples

## Cons

- Less detailed than practitioner manuals
- Some examples are high-level

## Best for

Teams and leaders new to Scrum who need a practical introduction.

## Alternatives

- Essential Scrum
- Scrum Guide
