# Scrum.org YouTube Channel

## Description

A high-quality video library from Scrum.org covering Scrum fundamentals, events, and practice.

## Why we recommend it

It offers concise, credible explanations that help teams and leaders reinforce their understanding of Scrum without relying on anecdotal advice.

## Pros

- Strong credibility and alignment with the framework
- Good for quick learning and refreshers

## Cons

- Less broad than general Agile video libraries
- Some content is more instructional than narrative

## Best for

Scrum practitioners, Scrum Masters, and learners wanting reliable foundational videos.

## Alternatives

- Atlassian Agile Coach
- Agile Alliance YouTube Channel
