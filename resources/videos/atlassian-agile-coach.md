# Atlassian Agile Coach

## Description

A practical video collection from Atlassian covering Agile delivery, coaching habits, and team practices.

## Why we recommend it

It offers clear, applied explanations for teams trying to improve their workflow without getting lost in theory.

## Pros

- Strong practical examples
- Useful for both leaders and practitioners

## Cons

- Some content is product-oriented
- Less framework-centric than Scrum-specific material

## Best for

Teams adopting Agile practices and leaders supporting continuous improvement.
