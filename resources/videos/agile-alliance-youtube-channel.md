# Agile Alliance YouTube Channel

## Description

A curated set of talks and interviews on Agile values, practices, and organizational change.

## Why we recommend it

It gives listeners access to experienced practitioners and thoughtful conversations that deepen Agile understanding beyond the basics.

## Pros

- Broad perspective on Agile adoption
- Strong exposure to practitioner experience

## Cons

- Content can be uneven in depth
- Some talks are more reflective than operational

## Best for

Leaders, coaches, and practitioners wanting a wider perspective on Agile practice.

## Alternatives

- Scrum.org YouTube Channel
- Atlassian Agile Coach
