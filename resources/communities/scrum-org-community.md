# Scrum.org Community

## Description

A community hub for Scrum practitioners to engage with events, discussions, and professional learning.

## Why we recommend it

It offers a credible place for practitioners to connect with peers, deepen their understanding, and stay close to Scrum developments.

## Pros

- Strong alignment with the Scrum framework
- Good for ongoing learning and connection

## Cons

- Less broad than general Agile communities
- Some engagement depends on active local participation

## Best for

Scrum practitioners, coaches, and leaders building their professional network.

## Alternatives

- Agile Alliance
- Local Agile meetups
