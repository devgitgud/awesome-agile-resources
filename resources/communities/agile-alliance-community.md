# Agile Alliance Community

## Description

A community-oriented hub for Agile practitioners to connect, learn, and exchange ideas across disciplines.

## Why we recommend it

It gives practitioners a credible place to engage with peers, discover events, and participate in the broader Agile conversation.

## Pros

- Strong community credibility
- Useful for networking and learning from peers

## Cons

- Participation varies by region and topic
- Some resources are broader than a single team context

## Best for

Practitioners, coaches, and leaders looking for ongoing community connection and peer learning.

## Alternatives

- Scrum.org Community
- Scrum Alliance
