# Scrum Alliance

## Description

A well-established community and professional network for Scrum practitioners, trainers, and organizations.

## Why we recommend it

It offers a credible place to connect with experienced practitioners and explore Scrum practices in a structured, mature community context.

## Pros

- Strong credibility in the Scrum ecosystem
- Useful for networking and professional development

## Cons

- Some content is tied to certification offerings
- Can be more structured than informal peer communities

## Best for

Scrum practitioners, coaches, and teams seeking a formal professional community.

## Alternatives

- Scrum.org Community
- Agile Alliance
