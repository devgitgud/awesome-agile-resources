# Scrum Master Toolbox Podcast

## Description

A practitioner-focused podcast on Scrum, facilitation, leadership, and team effectiveness.

## Why we recommend it

It offers grounded conversations that connect Agile theory with the day-to-day realities of coaching teams.

## Pros

- Strong practical perspective
- Useful for Scrum Masters and team leaders

## Cons

- Less structured than a course
- Some episodes are highly context-specific

## Best for

Scrum Masters, Agile coaches, and practitioners seeking real-world lessons.

## Alternatives

- Agile for Humans
- The Agile Coaching Podcast
