# Agile for Humans

## Description

A thoughtful podcast series exploring Agile, product thinking, collaboration, and organizational change.

## Why we recommend it

It helps listeners think beyond tools and ceremonies toward healthier team dynamics and better product outcomes.

## Pros

- Strong emphasis on human systems
- Good for leaders and product professionals

## Cons

- Not always focused on implementation mechanics
- Some episodes are more conceptual than tactical

## Best for

Leaders, coaches, and professionals interested in Agile mindset and organizational health.

## Alternatives

- Scrum Master Toolbox Podcast
- The Agile Coaching Podcast
