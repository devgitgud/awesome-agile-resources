# The Agile Coaching Podcast

## Description

A focused podcast about Agile coaching, team development, and the human side of organizational change.

## Why we recommend it

It provides rich perspectives for coaches and leaders navigating complex transformations without losing sight of reflection and growth.

## Pros

- Useful for coaching practice
- Strong emphasis on real-world change challenges

## Cons

- Some episodes are niche in their perspective
- Less suitable for beginners seeking a simple roadmap

## Best for

Agile coaches, managers, and practitioners supporting team transformation.

## Alternatives

- Scrum Master Toolbox Podcast
- Agile for Humans
